Frequently Asked Questions

How can I convert JPG to PDF?

Upload your JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP images, arrange them in the order you want, choose your PDF settings and click Convert to PDF. Your PDF will then be ready to download.

Is this JPG to PDF converter free?

Yes. You can use the JPG to PDF converter to create PDF files from your images without installing desktop software.

Can I convert multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. You can upload multiple images and combine them into a single PDF document. You can also reorder the images before conversion.

Can I rotate images before converting them?

Yes. Rotate individual images before creating the PDF. You can also reorder or remove images from the preview area.

Can I choose the PDF page size?

Yes. You can choose A4, Letter or the original image size. Portrait and landscape orientation options are also available.

Can I reduce the PDF file size?

Yes. The image quality setting provides high, medium and lower-quality options. Choosing a lower quality can help create a smaller PDF.

Can I add page numbers to the PDF?

Yes. You can choose whether page numbers should be displayed on the PDF pages before conversion.

Do I need to install software?

No. The converter works through your web browser, so you do not need a separate desktop application for the conversion.
